# Volux Gamification System Rules

## 1. Points System
- **Volunteering Hour:** +2 points per hour.
- **Positive Organization Evaluation:** +5 points.
- **Positive Team Leader Evaluation:** +5 points.
- **Attending Training/Workshop:** +10-20 points.
- **Bonus Activities:** Admin-defined.

## 2. Levels System
- **Bronze:** 0 – 199 points (New volunteer).
- **Silver:** 200 – 599 points (Active volunteer).
- **Gold:** 600 – 1199 points (Outstanding volunteer).
- **Platinum:** 1200+ points (Elite / Leadership volunteer).

## 3. Badges System
### 3.1 Basic Badges
- **First Opportunity:** First completed opportunity.
- **Active Volunteer:** 3 completed opportunities.
- **Level Silver:** Reach silver level.
- **Level Gold:** Reach gold level.
- **Level Platinum:** Reach platinum level.

### 3.2 Achievement Badges
- **Opportunity Finisher:** 10 completed opportunities.
- **Impact Maker:** 500+ total points.
- **Consistency Star:** Active for 3 consecutive months.
- **Community Helper:** Continuous platform engagement.

### 3.3 Leadership & Advanced Badges
- **Trusted Volunteer:** Completed 15 opportunities.
- **Change Maker:** Significant impact (Admin approval).
- **Elite Member:** Reach 1000 points.

## 4. Leaderboard
- Global and Monthly leaderboards.
- Ranking based on: Total Points, Number of Completed Opportunities, Number of Volunteering Hours.
